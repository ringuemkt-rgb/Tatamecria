# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-07

### Academic research release

- reframed NeuroJitsu as pre-validation motor-control research software;
- narrowed the primary public scope to transparent movement and postural-control measurement;
- added laboratory validation protocol;
- added measurement dictionary and known limitations;
- added reproducibility guidance;
- documented potential NEMO/UEL research alignment without implying affiliation;
- retained HRV, action-recognition, language-agent and WiFi-CSI modules as explicit research extensions;
- updated Brazil research timezone and project metadata;
- preserved privacy-first and human-oversight constraints.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-07-16

### Motion Intelligence

- whole-body contracts for body, hands, feet and facial points;
- transparent angle, range, trajectory, jerk, bilateral difference and trunk metrics;
- regional quality, occlusion, missing-frame and attribution-confidence controls;
- temporal technique-phase ontology;
- Supervision adapters and deterministic zone events.

### Governance

- facial identification excluded from the operational path;
- heavy or restricted-license models remain decoupled from the core;
- tracking metadata cannot contain names or direct identifiers.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-07-16

- deterministic core with Pydantic contracts;
- session state machine;
- kinematic and HRV utilities;
- transactional storage with integrity checks;
- SQLCipher option;
- JSON/HTML reports;
- FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker, CI and tests.
