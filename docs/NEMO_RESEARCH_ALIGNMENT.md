# Potential NEMO/UEL Research Alignment

> **No affiliation or endorsement is implied.** This document describes potential scientific fit for discussion with a prospective supervisor/laboratory.

## Why the fit is technically plausible

NeuroJitsu's current research core overlaps with domains commonly addressed in motor-neuroscience and biomechanics laboratories:

- motor control;
- postural control and balance;
- motor learning;
- movement variability;
- kinematics;
- measurement reliability;
- low-cost movement technology.

For a laboratory such as NEMO/UEL, the value of NeuroJitsu is **not** that it replaces laboratory equipment. The value is that it creates a testable low-cost measurement layer that can be compared with reference instrumentation.

## Research question 1 — criterion agreement

Can NeuroJitsu markerless variables reproduce reference kinematic measures during standardized motor tasks?

Candidate outputs:

- selected joint angles;
- range of motion;
- trajectory variables;
- trunk inclination.

## Research question 2 — postural control

Which video-derived features show acceptable reliability and meaningful agreement with laboratory balance/postural-control measures?

This question should be co-designed around the laboratory's validated instrumentation and protocols.

## Research question 3 — occlusion in grappling

How much measurement quality is lost when two participants interact in standing and ground grappling tasks?

Outputs:

- usable-frame percentage;
- landmark completeness;
- identity-uncertainty duration;
- single- vs multi-camera completeness.

## Research question 4 — longitudinal adapted-BJJ research

After technical validation, can the platform provide complementary longitudinal metrics during an adapted-BJJ intervention?

The primary scientific outcome should remain an established motor measure. NeuroJitsu should initially be a complementary measurement tool.

## What the project brings to a laboratory

- existing Python research code;
- synthetic test data;
- reproducible metric functions;
- validity/invalidity metadata;
- privacy-first architecture;
- explicit validation roadmap;
- grappling-specific occlusion problem;
- a concrete path from bench validation to applied adapted-sport research.

## What the project needs from a laboratory

- methodological supervision;
- selection of reference tasks;
- reference instrumentation;
- camera/calibration protocol;
- statistical validation plan;
- ethics/data-governance review;
- scientific judgment about which variables are worth retaining.

This is intentionally framed as a collaboration opportunity, not a finished validated system.
